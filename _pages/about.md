I am a Ph.D. student in the [Chem/Bioinformatics Lab](https://sites.google.com/view/cau-chembioinfo/home)
(advisor: Prof. Yoonji Lee) at the College of Pharmacy, Chung-Ang University.

My research focuses on **structure-aware artificial intelligence for drug discovery** —
from protein–ligand modeling to generative biomolecular design. I am broadly interested
in computational chemistry, bioinformatics, and applying AI to early-stage medicinal chemistry.

Before my Ph.D., I received my B.S. in Life Science and M.S. in the College of Pharmacy,
both at Chung-Ang University, and worked as a researcher in physiology and chem/bioinformatics labs.

Feel free to reach out via [email](mailto:hongryuly@gmail.com) or take a look at my
[Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=JyuXRAMAAAAJ&hl=ko).
